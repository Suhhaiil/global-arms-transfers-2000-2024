# Data Sources and References

1. **Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI)**
   - **Database**: SIPRI Arms Transfers Database
   - **Indicator**: Trend Indicator Values (TIV), expressed in millions of constant 1990 USD.
   - **Coverage**: 2000–2024 conventional weapons transfers (aircraft, armored vehicles, artillery, radar systems, missiles, and naval vessels).
   - **Portal / Documentation**: [SIPRI Arms Transfers Programme](https://www.sipri.org/databases/armstransfers)

2. **The World Bank (World Development Indicators - WDI)**
   - **Indicator `MS.MIL.XPRT.KD`**: Arms exports (SIPRI trend indicator values).
   - **Indicator `MS.MIL.MPRT.KD`**: Arms imports (SIPRI trend indicator values).
   - **Source Organization**: Stockholm International Peace Research Institute (SIPRI), Arms Transfers Programme.
   - **Portal**: [World Bank Open Data](https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/MS.MIL.XPRT.KD)

3. **Methodological Note on SIPRI TIV**
   - Figures are expressed in Trend Indicator Values (TIVs) based on the known unit production costs of a core set of weapons, measuring the volume of military resources transferred rather than commercial financial transaction values.
   - Deliveries between 0 and 0.5 million SIPRI TIV are officially recorded as `0`.
